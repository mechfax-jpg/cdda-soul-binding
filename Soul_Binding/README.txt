Soul Binding
============
Bind Essence lets you permanently stamp enchantments onto
any worn item, for the low low payment of some mana dust, some gems and a small part of your soul! 


GETTING STARTED
---------------
Somewhere out there is a single page describing a forbidden rite.

Once paid, it cannot be undone.

Are you binding items to yourself?

Or are you binding yourself to items?

They say this is a cursed and taboo and the price is more then just gems and dust.

Some say you have to dip your very soul in hell to gain such power..

END GAME STUFF
-----------------------------------------------
  CAPSTONE: Warrior's Oath, Mage's Communion, or Ranger's Vow.
           a huge build-defining bonus with a drawback.

  LYOIDS BEACON:  a mark and recall styled system bound to one of five independent slots.
    Wear a marked item and it pulls you to that slot's last anchor.
    Take a marked item off and it re-anchors the slot to wherever you're
    standing. No spell required after the initial binding, just put
    it on, or take it off.

THE SOUL TOLL
--------------
Every binding costs you something beyond dust and gems: a lasting,
cumulative toll on your own body, worse the more you've
bound. It fades slowly over time if you stop casting. This is
deliberate the payoff is real, but so is the price. 
Better prepare carefully.
