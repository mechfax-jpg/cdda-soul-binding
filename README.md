# SOUL BINDING

Bind Essence lets you permanently stamp enchantments onto
any worn item, for the low, low payment of some mana dust,
some gems, and a small part of your soul!

## REQUIREMENTS

Magiclysm

## DEVELOPMENT VERSION

MOD VERSION: 0.7

## COMPATIBILITY

This mod is intended to work across CDDA versions as long as
the Magiclysm systems it depends upon remain compatible.

Developed and tested with:
Ito-1 DEV

## INSTALLATION

Download the repository as a ZIP and place the mod in your
Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead mods directory.

## GETTING STARTED

Somewhere out there is a single page describing a forbidden rite.

Once paid, it cannot be undone.

Are you binding items to yourself?

Or are you binding yourself to items?

They say this is cursed and taboo, and the price is more
than just gems and dust.

Some say you have to dip your very soul in hell to gain such power...

## END GAME STUFF

CAPSTONE

Warrior's Oath, Mage's Communion, or Ranger's Vow.

A huge, build-defining bonus with a drawback.

LYOIDS BEACON

A mark-and-recall style system bound to one of five
independent slots.

Wear a marked item and it pulls you to that slot's last anchor.

Take a marked item off and it re-anchors the slot to wherever
you're standing.

No spell is required after the initial binding. Just put it on,
or take it off.
